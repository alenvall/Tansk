# What's new in TANSK 2026

The title screen shows this file ("What's new" and "Coming up"). Newest
first. Each release is a `## YYYY-MM-DD Title` heading with `-` bullets,
written for players. The `## Coming up` section is what's being built next.

## Coming up

- More height on the older maps.
- Tuning from your idea box posts.
- Your ideas from the idea box: we read everything.

## 2026-11-19 Things break now

- Shacks, fences, stalls, tents, towers and poles take damage, get holed, and come down. Water towers topple.
- Drive fast through fences, stalls and tents to flatten them.
- Bombs, mortars and fuel blasts dig real craters you can drive in and out of.
- What's new, Coming up and an idea box on the title screen.

## 2026-11-19 The Gorge, power-ups and the Workshop manual

- New map: The Gorge, a deep winding canyon on three levels.
- Timed power-ups glow at a few spots on every map: Overcharge (double damage), Nitro, Bubble, Gatling and Magnet.
- The Workshop manual: every track, branch, gun, gadget and pickup, with the real numbers. On the title, in the lobby and on the pause card, plus your current build.
- Spring pads that fling you across the map, and fuel trailers that go up in a fireball.
- Shields and shells look solid now, and explosions cost your computer less.

## 2026-11-19 Kill-cam and Play of the match

- When you're wrecked, the camera swings to whoever did it: their name, gun and build. Fire skips it.
- Before the podium, the best moment of the match replays: the biggest multi-kill or damage burst.
- Bots have personalities now (Sniper, Brawler, Scavenger, Hunter) and work in small squads.

## 2026-11-19 Phones, tablets and the crew yard

- Play on phones and tablets: touch controls and layouts made for small screens.
- Play with friends: host a crew yard, send the invite link, ready up, pick the rules, then vote on the next map after each match. If the host leaves, the next crew takes over.
- Your Garage pick (chassis, paint, emblem and the new horns) is saved and follows you into every game.
- Props between the camera and your tank fade away, the camera keeps out of walls, and the start of a match no longer swings the view about.
- Distinct times of day, clearer shadows, tighter smoke, and every sound now plays without hitches.

## 2026-11-19 The Rust Belt and cartoon tanks

- New map: The Rust Belt, a scrapyard city in a dry canyon.
- Tanks bounce, squash and rock like cartoons.
- Bots stay off cliff bases and get themselves unstuck; Convoy is better balanced.
- Wellhead (hold the derricks) and Last Crew Rolling (no respawns, a storm wall closing in) join the modes.
- Tips as you play, a controls card and a How to play page.

## 2026-11-19 The Refinery, Convoy and the Land-Ship

- New map: The Refinery, with a tank farm that goes up in chain reactions.
- Convoy: escort the fuel tanker to the depot, then stop theirs.
- Raid gets a new boss, THE LAND-SHIP. Music in the game, and horns.
- Team Brawl online, gamepad support, quality presets and the first kill-cam.

## 2026-11-19 TANSK 2026, first playable

- The reimagined TANSK: hand-welded desert tanks that grow as you grab scrap and fall apart as you get shot.
- Scrap Brawl, Team Brawl and Raid (co-op waves) on The Sink, online or against bots.
- Sandstorms, garage chassis, and damage you can see on every tank.

## 2026-11-18 Back from 2013

- The original 2013 TANSK rewritten for the web, and a first 3D view of it.
