# Sound licences

Every `.ogg` in this folder is cut, layered, filtered and re-encoded (Ogg Vorbis, 48 kHz) from
recordings in the **Sonniss #GameAudioGDC bundles** (2015 to 2019), downloaded with plain HTTP
range requests from the official mirror that sonniss.com links for those years
(`https://ftpmirror.your.org/pub/misc/sonniss*`, listed on https://sonniss.com/gameaudiogdc).

**Licence:** the Sonniss #GameAudioGDC Bundle Licensing Agreement (the `License.pdf` /
`Licensing.pdf` inside each bundle; summary at https://sonniss.com/gdc-bundle-license/):
a worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to use the sounds in unlimited commercial and
non-commercial projects, including games, **without attribution**, and to edit them and
distribute them as part of the project. Not allowed: selling or redistributing the sounds on
their own, claiming authorship, or using them to train AI/ML models. No attribution is
required; the authors are credited below anyway. No CC-BY, CC-BY-NC or ShareAlike material is used.

Sounds not listed here (the coilgun charge, shell and bomb whistles, ricochet, countdown beeps,
War Rig roar and wave horn, the trumpet and klaxon horns, and all music) are synthesized in
`crates/arena-audio` and have no third-party source.

Columns: the shipped file; each source recording (author / library, bundle, file inside the
bundle zip, the time range used, and the processing).
